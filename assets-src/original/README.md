# Nook Kampala — 42-asset concept collection

A new fictional apartments-and-stays platform concept. This collection contains 30 core property photos, 6 property-specific amenity photos, 2 hero images, 3 logo assets and 1 social cover.

## Files and formats

Photographs and social cover are high-quality WebP (quality 96) at original generated dimensions, with no cropping or resizing. Branding remains PNG. Source PNG imagery was generated using the built-in image-generation tool; the ZIP contains web-friendly derivatives. Dimensions and sizes are recorded in asset-manifest.json. The logo sources are raster visual references, not vector masters or exported favicon.ico files. They have solid backgrounds, not transparency.

## Brand

Name: Nook Kampala. Descriptor: Apartments & stays across Kampala.
Suggested headline: Find your place in Kampala.
Palette: limestone cream #F4EFE5, deep olive #343E32, charcoal and restrained terracotta. Pair refined editorial serif typography with highly readable UI sans serif. Name/domain/trademark availability has not been checked; this is a portfolio identity, not a registered-brand assertion.

## Hero and motion

Use heroes/nook-desktop-hero.webp on desktop and heroes/nook-mobile-hero.webp for deliberate mobile composition. Keep headline, search controls and CTAs live in HTML. The desktop composition may need a restrained gradient or adjacent text panel for contrast; test actual typography over it. Do not use the flattened social cover as a website hero.

Recommended GSAP direction: short headline reveal, elegant photograph-mask reveal, restrained scale/parallax and transitions between property/amenity scenes. Animate a few intentional moments. Keep search, filters, date picker and booking actions stable. Respect reduced motion and simplify on phones. These are flattened photographs, not depth maps or separately movable architectural layers. Do not distort walls, water or furniture for fake 3D effects.

## Quality and coherence

References and fixed material palettes were used across galleries. These are visually related concept images, not measured floor plans or a geometrically exact digital twin. Do not imply every window, viewpoint or room connection is physically verified. Skylines and outlooks are imagined, not proof of a real view from a named Kampala neighbourhood. Check crops and image details at actual display size before publishing.

## Boundaries for the eventual demo

All six properties, interiors, amenities and brand assets are fictional independent concepts. Do not pair these photos with real apartment names from the research list. Do not add real pins, phone numbers, ratings, reviews, availability, prices or host identities unless verified or explicitly labelled illustrative. No real bookings or payments. Sample reservation/viewing journeys should conclude with honest local demo confirmations.

Potential future pages: landing, apartment search/filter results, property detail gallery, saved stays, short-stay enquiry, long-term viewing request, comparison, neighbourhood exploration and contact. This asset kit does not implement those pages or provide a master implementation prompt.
